## Method
A bot plays full runs of the real `Game`. It steers with BFS to the food over cells that aren't body. Two profiles:
- **greedy:** food only.
- **careful:** also avoids cells within 70 px of an enemy when a path exists.

Upgrades are chosen at random unless a preference is set (`--upgrades prefer:<key>`). Each run is seeded.

**Limits:** a bot is not a person. It reacts with no latency, never mis-steers, and has a fixed strategy. Treat the numbers as relative comparisons between configurations, not as how hard the game is for a human. A bot-confirmed finding should still be re-checked by hand.

## 1. Speed and profile (30 runs each, random upgrades)

| Profile | Speed | Deaths: enemy / self | Median wave (min–max) | Median score | Median hits taken |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| careful | Relaxed | 30 / 0 | 7 (4–11) | 1265 | 6 |
| careful | Normal | 30 / 0 | 8 (6–21) | 1735 | 6 |
| careful | Fast | 27 / 3 | 7 (5–14) | 1730 | 6 |
| careful | Blazing | 26 / 4 | 8 (5–10) | 2160 | 6 |
| greedy | Relaxed | 30 / 0 | 6 (4–19) | 1040 | 6 |
| greedy | Normal | 30 / 0 | 6 (4–19) | 1120 | 5.5 |
| greedy | Fast | 30 / 0 | 6 (4–8) | 1155 | 6 |
| greedy | Blazing | 30 / 0 | 5 (3–8) | 925 | 6 |

Observations:
- 233 of 240 runs ended from enemy damage. The 7 self-collisions were all careful runs at Fast or Blazing, which is plausibly bot error.
- Starting speed barely changes how far a run gets. It mostly changes score.
- Playing carefully gains about 2 waves over playing greedily.

## 2. Hits taken per run (all 240 runs)

| Hits taken | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runs | 1 | 3 | 2 | 85 | 86 | 44 | 18 | 1 |

Among the 233 runs that ended from enemy damage, hits taken by number of Extra Heart upgrades: 0 hearts 5.0, 1 heart 6.0, 2 hearts 7.0, 3 hearts 8.0. Every heart is exactly one more hit.

*Correction (PR review of [TECH-008](../technical/TECH-008-playtest-bot-miscounts.md)):* an earlier version of the bot counted wall and self deaths as a hit and missed food pickups that coincided with a kill. Tables 1 and 2 were re-run after the fix. Medians barely moved; the 7 self-collision runs were the only ones affected. See [GP-006](../gameplay/GP-006-runs-end-by-hp-attrition.md).

## 3. Upgrade preference experiment (careful, Normal, 60 runs each)
The policy takes the named upgrade whenever it is offered and picks randomly otherwise.

| Policy | Mean wave | Median | SD | Std. error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| prefer big_bullet | 14.35 | 11.5 | 6.91 | 0.89 |
| prefer faster_fire | 10.00 | 8 | 4.98 | 0.64 |
| random (control) | 8.73 | 8 | 3.27 | 0.42 |
| prefer extra_heart | 8.33 | 8 | 2.16 | 0.28 |
| prefer thick_skin | 7.58 | 7 | 1.72 | 0.22 |
| prefer swift_snake | 7.17 | 7 | 1.37 | 0.18 |
| prefer piercing_shot | 7.20 | 7 | 1.39 | 0.18 |

The random and Piercing Shot rows were re-run after the review fixes (previously 8.85 and 7.15); the conclusion did not change. The other rows are pre-fix. The fixes do not touch their upgrades, but runs that pick randomly when the preferred upgrade isn't offered can include a Piercing Shot pick, so those rows are not strictly unaffected.

The earlier approach of correlating "had upgrade X" with final wave was **discarded**: runs that survive longer see more upgrade offers, so that comparison is biased toward whatever was picked late. The preference experiment avoids that.

Findings: [GP-005](../gameplay/GP-005-big-bullet-dominates.md), [GP-007](../gameplay/GP-007-four-upgrades-do-not-help.md).

## Reproduce
```bash
python tools/playtest.py --runs 30 --speed Normal --profile careful --seed 1
python tools/playtest.py --runs 60 --speed Normal --profile careful --upgrades prefer:big_bullet --seed 7
```
The second command can take a few minutes because strong builds survive up to the 600 s cap.
